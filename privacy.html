Data Protection

Membership Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The European Society of Teleradiology (ESTeR) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Italian legislation.

1. Data Controller

European Society of Teleradiology (ESTeR)
Via Garibaldi 110
98122 Messina, Italy
Email: secretariat@society-teleradiology.org

2. Personal Data Collected

When applying for membership, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Country
  • Profession or professional role
  • Institution or organisation (optional)
  • Optional newsletter preference

We also maintain administrative information, including application date, membership number, membership status, Board decisions, approval date and relevant communication preferences.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Membership administration: We process applications, submit them to the Board for consideration, maintain the membership register and administer membership under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Legal and governance obligations: We maintain records where required by applicable law under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Other necessary governance processing may rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), where applicable.

Optional newsletters: With your consent, we may send scientific news, educational announcements and information about ESTeR activities under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

4. Membership Approval

Membership applications are reviewed by the ESTeR Board of Directors in accordance with the Society's Statute.

The Board may approve or reject applications. Rejections are communicated with reasons, and applicants may exercise the appeal rights provided in the Statute. Membership decisions are not made solely through automated processing.

5. Data Storage and Security

Membership records are maintained using Baserow, a database platform providing cloud data hosting in Germany.

Access is restricted to authorised persons involved in membership administration and governance. Appropriate organisational and technical safeguards are applied to protect personal information.

6. Disclosure and International Transfers

Personal data may be accessible to authorised ESTeR Board members and administrative personnel and to technical service providers acting on behalf of ESTeR.

ESTeR does not sell membership information or provide membership contact lists to corporate members for their own marketing activities.

Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, ESTeR will ensure that an appropriate legal transfer mechanism and safeguards are in place, as required by the GDPR.

7. Data Retention

  • Active members: Data is retained throughout membership.
  • Rejected applications: Data is normally deleted or anonymised within 12 months of the rejection decision.
  • Resigned members: Data is normally deleted or anonymised within 12 months of resignation.
  • Newsletter consent: Consent records are retained as necessary to manage preferences and demonstrate compliance.

Limited information may be retained for longer where required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability or object to processing.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact: secretariat@society-teleradiology.org .

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

9. Updates

ESTeR may update this notice when its membership processes, service providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published here.
